System Design

System Architecture

Six stages between intent and result. Every interface is specified around one fixed condition: the one-way signal delay between Earth and Mars is 4 to 22 minutes, and no part of the chain can assume a real-time response.

  1. 01

    Human Operator

    Defines objectives, constraints, priorities, and acceptance criteria. Retains authority over every irreversible action.

  2. 02

    Mission AI

    Translates objectives into executable tasks, allocates them across the fleet, monitors execution, and manages faults within its assigned decision class.

  3. 03

    Deep Space Communication

    Uplink and downlink scheduled against limited windows. Traffic is prioritised by information value rather than by request order.

  4. 04

    Mars Relay Network

    Orbital and surface relays extend local coverage and buffer traffic between contact periods.

  5. 05

    Autonomous Robotic Fleet

    Specialised avatars execute tasks on the surface, respond to hazards within seconds, and negotiate reallocation among themselves.

  6. 06

    Scientific Operations

    Measurements, samples, and constructed infrastructure, returned with provenance and the decision record that produced them.

Authority flows downward; evidence flows back on the same delayed channel. The operator reviews outcomes and authorises the next objective — the operator never steers. Mission AI is the layer that makes this practical: it holds the fleet's shared model of the mission and decides how work is distributed when Earth cannot be consulted in time.

Design Rationale

Why MAP?

Six properties that distinguish this architecture from single-vehicle remote operation.

Human-in-the-Loop

Every decision of consequence remains with the operator; only the steering is delegated.

Mission AI Coordination

A single coordination layer holds the shared world model and allocates work across the fleet.

Goal-Based Control

Earth transmits the objective and its constraints; the fleet determines the method on site.

Fleet Intelligence

Specialised units bid for tasks and reassign them when a unit degrades or a plan is invalidated.

Scalable Architecture

Layer interfaces are defined independently of unit count, so the fleet can grow without redesign.

Earth → Moon → Mars Roadmap

A staged verification sequence with explicit criteria for progressing from one environment to the next.

Development Sequence

Technology Roadmap

Indicative phasing. No phase is funded, scheduled, or under way; each is contingent on the results of the one before it.

  1. 2026–2030

    Concept Demonstration

    Simulation of the full command chain under injected latency, with reproducible benchmarks.

  2. 2030–2035

    Lunar Demonstration

    Supervisory command exercised on real hardware at genuine, if short, light-time delay.

  3. 2035–2045

    Mars Deployment

    The validated architecture applied to surface operations at full interplanetary delay.

  4. Objective

    Robotic Infrastructure

    Landing pads, power, and stored consumables emplaced and maintained without a crew present.

  5. Objective

    Support for Human Missions

    Routine surface work carried by supervised avatars, reserving crew time for what requires people.
